Frequently asked questions

A closer look.

What is FitMeNow™?

FitMeNow™ is MeNow’s patent-pending, non-peptide small-molecule approach designed to act directly on the GLP-1 receptor, with potential applications in satiety-focused food, beverages and supplements.

Does MeNow sell the ingredient?

No. MeNow offers licensing of the new ingredient application and associated intellectual property, not ingredient sales or supply. The underlying ingredient is commercially available through established flavor-industry suppliers.

What does the license cover?

The opportunity concerns MeNow’s patent-pending ingredient application and intellectual property. The specific application, territory, rights and commercial terms are defined with each prospective partner.

How is this different from indirect GLP-1 support?

FitMeNow™ is designed to act directly on the GLP-1 receptor. Indirect approaches aim to stimulate the body’s release of GLP-1 first. This distinction describes the intended mechanism, not demonstrated superiority in speed, strength or duration.

Is FitMeNow™ a prescription GLP-1 medicine?

No. FitMeNow™ is presented as a licensing opportunity for consumer-product development. It is not a substitute for prescription treatment, and the supplied consumer feedback does not establish equivalence to prescription medicines.

What does the flavor-use history mean for a new application?

The ingredient’s established flavor-use history provides a starting point for development. It does not automatically establish suitability for a satiety application at every use level or in every product and market. Those requirements need their own assessment.

What formulation information is available?

A technical meeting can clarify the information available for your intended format, including use-level considerations, sensory requirements, processing conditions and evaluation needs. Suitability must be established for the specific formulation.

How can we get started?

Book a meeting with your intended product format, target market and development goals. Together we can review the opportunity, available evidence and next steps for evaluation and licensing.
